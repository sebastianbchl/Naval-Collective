FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need previous robotics experience?

No. Curiosity and commitment matter more than previous experience.

How much time should I commit?

Most members attend one build session and contribute flexible project time each week.

Can non-engineers join?

Yes. Communications, operations, partnerships, events, and documentation are also important.
